Privacy Policy — Periodic Table Arena (Periodic Lab)

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

Package ID: com.periodictable.arena

1. Information We Collect

Periodic Table Arena ("Periodic Lab") does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, contacts, analytics, or usage telemetry.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Preferences you choose inside the app—such as Light/Dark theme, sound settings, and any custom chemistry mnemonics you write—are stored strictly locally on your own device using standard browser/WebView local storage. This information never leaves your device and can be cleared anytime from your device settings or inside the app.

3. Children's & Students' Privacy

Periodic Table Arena is an educational chemistry learning tool safe for learners of all ages. Because zero personal information is collected or transmitted, children's privacy is completely protected in compliance with COPPA and global privacy standards.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Ads

This application contains no third-party advertising networks, trackers, or external analytics SDKs, and functions 100% offline.

5. Contact

For questions or support regarding this application, visit the project repository at https://github.com/smartstopwach/perodic-table.
